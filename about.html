River
Version 0.1.0 · development build

River is a free and open source app intended to be an online or offline user interface for the best freely available English Bibles and original language Bible study tools.

Verse-numbering alignment data in this app is derived from two sources: STEPBible's TVTMS dataset (© Tyndale House Cambridge / STEPBible.org, CC BY 4.0) and the AndBible/jsword project's Vulgate versification mappings (LGPL 2.1+).

Finding which glosses should be lumped together is done using NLTK's WordNetLemmatizer (Apache 2.0).

Related Greek words (word families and compounds) in the Occurrences pane are derived from the derivation notes in Open Scriptures' Strong's Greek Dictionary (text public domain; XML © Open Scriptures, CC BY-SA 4.0), G. Abbott-Smith's 1904 A Manual Greek Lexicon of the New Testament (public domain; digital edition from translatable-exegetical-tools/Abbott-Smith), and etymology and derived-term data from Wiktionary contributors, extracted by wiktextract and distributed via kaikki.org (CC BY-SA 4.0).

Cross-references were built from two sources: the marginal cross-reference apparatus of the original 1582/1610 Douay-Rheims Bible (public domain), and openbible.info's Cross References dataset (CC BY 4.0), which traces back to the public-domain Treasury of Scripture Knowledge and is filtered here to high-confidence, community-voted entries.
